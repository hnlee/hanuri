# Donate

Hanuri Korean School strives to make this school open to all families whose children want to be a part of a caring community, and to create the best kind of experience for their children. We can do this with the generosity of families, community members, businesses, and other community institutions.

Your tax-deductible gift will help Hanuri Korean School:

- Inspire children to develop pride in their Korean heritage and culture.
- Provide exceptional teachers and engaging classroom experience.
- Offer enriching cultural experiences, including traditional Korean drumming, dance, arts, and other hands-on cultural programming.
- Create a welcoming school where all families feel they belong.
- Build a strong financial foundation that will allow Hanuri Korean School to grow and serve families for years to come.

Contact us at [hanurikoreanschool@gmail.com](mailto:hanurikoreanschool@gmail.com) to learn how you can become a supporter of Hanuri Korean School.
