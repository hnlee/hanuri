# Registration

Children must be in grades K-12 and be at least 5 years of age at the start of the semester to enroll. We group students in classes primarily by age and secondarily by proficiency level to ensure they will be learning with and from their peers.

- **Fall semester:** The registration form opens on **July 31, 2026** and will remain open until **August 30, 2026**.
- **Spring semester:** The registration form opens on **January 9, 2027** and will remain open until **February 7, 2027**.

We welcome children who want to enroll after the start of the semester. If you want to register your child after the deadline, please contact us directly at [hanurikoreanschool@gmail.com](mailto:hanurikoreanschool@gmail.com).
