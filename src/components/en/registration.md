# Registration

**We are no longer accepting more students for fall semester.** Contact us at [hanurikoreanschool@gmail.com](mailto:hanurikoreanschool@gmail.com) to receive our announcement about spring semester registration.

- **Spring semester:** The registration form opens on **January 9, 2027** and will remain open until **February 7, 2027**.
