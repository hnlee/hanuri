# Why Hanuri Korean School?

## Students Learn

We provide age-appropriate Korean language instruction that builds confidence and fluency. We know that everyone has different levels of Korean language skills, and our teachers are able to work with and encourage their students to enjoy learning no matter where they start.

## Students Connect

Children build friendships while celebrating Korean culture, history and traditions. We recognize that some Korean American children are in spaces where they are the “only” Korean American or “one of a few” Korean Americans, whether that be in schools, neighborhood, or even in their family. Hanuri Korean School aims to be a space where they are one of many.

We serve students from ages 5 to 18, allowing them to create connections to peers that last throughout their school years and beyond.

## Students Belong

We strive to be an inclusive community where every family is welcomed and every child feels they belong. Our school brings together Korean American families from many backgrounds: first-, second-, third-, or fourth-generation families, multiracial and multiethnic families, and families with transracial adoptees. By learning alongside one another, our students gain a richer understanding of the many ways to be Korean American. We also welcome those who may not be ethnically Korean but have a deep curiosity about Korean language and culture.
