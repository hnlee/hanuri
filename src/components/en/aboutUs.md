# About Us

## Who We Are

Hanuri Korean School is a 501(c)3 non-profit organization whose mission is to provide an inclusive educational experience for children ages 5-18 to learn Korean language, culture, art, and history, instilling pride and a sense of belonging to our diverse Korean American community in the Chicago area.

## Our Story

Hanuri Korean School was founded by a group of dedicated parents who believed that the closing of their beloved Chicago-area Korean school should not mean the end of the community it had created: a welcoming place where Korean American children and youth from all backgrounds could learn, connect, and belong.

Parents believed this community was too important to lose.

Rather than let that community disappear, these families came together with a shared vision: to create a new school that preserves the warmth and inclusion they valued while offering new opportunities for children to learn and grow. From that vision, Hanuri Korean School was born.

## Our Name

**Hanuri (한우리)** means "our Korean community." The name combines the Korean words **han** (one, great, or big) and **uri** (we or us), expressing the idea of one united community. It reflects our belief that every child deserves a place to learn the Korean language, celebrate Korean culture, and develop a lifelong connection to the diverse Korean American community.

## Principal

**Meeyeon Yoon**, our principal, taught middle school in Korea before moving to the U.S. in 2016. She has taught at several Korean schools, including Truelight Korean School (참빛한국학교), Niles Korean School, and Bulta Korean School, before stepping into the principal role at Bulta. She believes in making Korean school a joyful place to learn. Her favorite food is kimchi stew.
